# Preschool Unfiltered: brand file

Source: the "Preschool Unfiltered" website artifact, which matches https://preschoolunfiltered.netlify.app (read 2026-10-31).

## Who we are
- Small TPT shop from Cambridge, Ontario. Made by a teacher in a real preschool classroom.
- Tagline: "Real preschool. Real resources. No fluff." / "real classrooms, no fluff"
- The test: "If it didn't work with real three- and four-year-olds, it doesn't go in the store."

## Promise to buyers
- Low prep: print, cut, play
- Big, simple pictures for little hands
- Laminate once, reuse every year
- Most resources under $3 (USD, set on TPT)

## Voice
Warm, plain and practical. Teacher to teacher. Short sentences, no hype, no edu-jargon.

## What we sell now
- Grade focus: Pre-K and Kindergarten, now stretching to Grade 2 (pumpkin crafts bundle is PreK to 2nd).
- Themes: seasonal (fall, Halloween, pumpkin week, Christmas), classroom management (Clean-Up Spinner, $3),
  inclusion/AAC (trick-or-treat picture boards with ASL-based signs, $1.50), games (Monster Matching Game, free),
  sub binder, patterns, colour sorting, colouring, bingo, gift tags.
- Formats: printables plus interactive web tools (spinners, games).
- Coming soon: 50 Pumpkin Crafts & Fall Activities (PreK to 2nd).

## Gaps worth watching
- Nothing yet for Grades 3 to 4.
- Few core-skill (phonics, math facts) products; most products are seasonal or classroom routines.
