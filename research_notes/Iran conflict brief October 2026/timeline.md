Research date: 2026-10-30. Sources are mostly live blogs (CBS, Al Jazeera, Iran International, Fox) and daily CTP-ISW "Iran Update" reports. Many claims come from a party to the conflict (IRGC, Houthis, Trump, Saudi-led coalition) and are attributed to them. Search snippets and fetched page summaries were the basis. ## What are the major dated events from ~Oct 16 to Oct 30, 2026?

### Takeaway
There were no confirmed major US or Israeli strikes on Iran in the window. The two weeks were a "neither war nor peace" standoff. Iran attacked shipping, including a cluster of tanker mine/projectile incidents in and near Hormuz on Oct 29–30. The US kept its naval blockade. Washington and Israel reportedly planned a short, intense strike campaign after the Nov 3 US midterms. Qatar, Pakistan, Saudi Arabia and Turkey stepped up mediation. On Oct 27 the UN Security Council was briefed on the IAEA's referral of Iran. The Houthi–Saudi war in Yemen and Saudi Arabia was the most kinetically active front.

## What is the current status (active fighting, ceasefire, negotiations) as of Oct 30, 2026?

### Takeaway
There is **no ceasefire in force**. Major US and Israeli strikes on Iran appear paused, but renewed strikes are reportedly being planned for after the Nov 3 US midterms. Active fighting continues at sea in and around Hormuz, where Iran is attacking shipping and the US blockade remains, and through proxies, mainly in Yemen and Saudi Arabia. Mediation led by Qatar, with Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, Turkey and Oman involved, is active but unresolved. Iran says it has not answered the Vance letter.

## What brief background (when/how this phase began) does a reader need?

### Takeaway
The US and Israel launched joint strikes on Iran on **Feb 28, 2026**, killing Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei. Iran retaliated and closed Hormuz. A Pakistan-mediated ceasefire began Apr 7–8, followed by failed Islamabad talks and a US naval blockade from Apr 13. A mid-June extension and the "Islamabad Memorandum" followed, but the ceasefire collapsed on Jul 8. Since late summer, the conflict has settled into a blockade and maritime struggle over Hormuz, with Houthi–Saudi war spreading in Yemen. Mojtaba Khamenei is now Supreme Leader.

### Cited Findings (background, before Oct 16)
- Feb 28, 2026: The US and Israel launched airstrikes, killing the supreme leader and other officials. Iran retaliated with missiles and drones and closed Hormuz. — [Wikipedia: 2026 Iran war ceasefire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_war_ceasefire); [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-donald-trump-qatar-mediators-new-proposal-end-war/). CTP-ISW's Oct 26 update dates Khamenei's killing to "March 2026," a **minor discrepancy**. — [CTP-ISW, Oct 26](https://www.criticalthreats.org/analysis/iran-update-october-26-2026)
- Apr 7–8: Pakistan mediated a two-week ceasefire. The Islamabad Talks on Apr 11–12 ended without agreement, and the US imposed a naval blockade on Apr 13. The ceasefire was extended on Apr 21. A 60-day extension came Jun 11–12, the Islamabad Memorandum on Jun 17, and the collapse on Jul 8, when fighting resumed. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_war_ceasefire). The article is flagged as needing an update and has internal inconsistencies.
- In May the Pentagon dropped the name "Operation Epic Fury" after Rubio declared the operation over. In late August, Trump and CENTCOM said the main Hormuz lanes were demined. In September, Iran proposed a seven-day ceasefire and phased Hormuz reopening plan after UNGA, which Trump rejected. — [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-donald-trump-qatar-mediators-new-proposal-end-war/)
- An Israel–Hezbollah ceasefire was agreed in June 2026. Saudi Arabia, Pakistan and Turkey signed the Mecca Joint Defense Pact in August 2026. The Houthi–Saudi war resumed in July 2026, and the Houthis have made territorial gains since Sep 9–10, including Mokha and the remaining Red Sea ports. — [CTP-ISW, Oct 26](https://www.criticalthreats.org/analysis/iran-update-october-26-2026); [CTP-ISW, Oct 29](https://www.criticalthreats.org/analysis/iran-update-october-29-2026)

- Early October included a Houthi strike on Riyadh airport, with a death toll reported as 13 or 17 (conflicting), and a tanker strike on Oct 9. — [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-trump-us-oil-gas-prices-strait-of-hormuz-midterms/)
- Prior context: the June 2025 "12-day war," in which the US and Israel struck Iran's nuclear program. — [CTP-ISW, Oct 26](https://www.criticalthreats.org/analysis/iran-update-october-26-2026)

### Inferences
- "This phase" began with the Jul 8 ceasefire collapse. By October it had become a lower-intensity standoff, with the blockade on one side and Iranian Hormuz interdiction on the other, rather than a sustained air campaign.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable account of the scale or duration of US/Israeli strikes after Jul 8, or of when they tapered off.

## What are reported casualty/damage figures, and who reports them?

### Takeaway
Reported figures vary widely by source and are mostly unverifiable. The Pentagon counts **23 US service-member deaths**. HRANA's compilation counts about **3,636 deaths in Iran**, including 1,701 civilians. Iran's Health Ministry reports at least 2,362 civilians killed. The US war cost is estimated at about $47B. Trump's claims about destroyed Iranian ships and aircraft are unverified and inconsistent.

### Cited Findings
- US deaths: 23 total (11 hostile, 12 non-hostile), per the Pentagon DCAS as of Oct 30. — [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-donald-trump-qatar-mediators-new-proposal-end-war/)
