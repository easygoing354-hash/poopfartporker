# Classroom Elf Calendar: December 2026 (Grades K–2)

`Classroom-Elf-Calendar-December-2026.pdf` is a 59-page, print-ready packet (US Letter):

- Cover with tips
- A Monday–Friday calendar: for each of the 16 school days (Dec 1–22), what the elf does and the idea, plus 6 bonus ideas
- A 3-page teacher guide (scene setup, morning activity, supplies)
- A props how-to page listing every day's laminate-and-reuse props
- One section per day: student printables, then props to laminate
  - Day 1: welcome letter (rules, naming, joke) and 6 elf doors with accessories
  - Day 16: goodbye letter and Nice List certificate

To rebuild: `node elf-calendar/build.js` (needs Playwright + Chromium).

Source files: `build.js` (content + layout), `art.js` / `art2.js` (drawings), `kit.js` (prop building blocks), `styles.js`.
Fonts: Fredoka and Patrick Hand (SIL Open Font License).
