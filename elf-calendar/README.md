# Classroom Elf Calendar: December 2026 (Grades K–2)

`Classroom-Elf-Calendar-December-2026.pdf` is a 40-page, print-ready packet (US Letter):

- Cover with tips
- A Monday–Friday calendar with a different elf idea for every school day, Dec 1–22
- A two-page teacher guide covering the scene setup, morning activity, and supplies for each day
- 18 printables, numbered to match the calendar
- Laminate & reuse props for every day (signs, speech bubbles, dry-erase boards, game pieces), plus bonus dry-erase signs

To rebuild: `node elf-calendar/build.js` (needs Playwright + Chromium).
Fonts: Fredoka and Patrick Hand (SIL Open Font License).
